Curriculum Progression

Designed for Every Stage of Curiosity

A step-by-step practical pathway introducing school students to modern technology without unnecessary complexity.

Grade 5

Creative Coding & 3D Basics

Building visual logic foundations, computational thinking, and spatial design through fun interactive environments.

  • Block-based logic & sequencing
  • Creative 3D spatial thinking
Grade 6

Electronics & Robotics Intro

Connecting software with hardware. Students explore how sensors detect the world and control dynamic motors.

  • Sensors, circuits & LED routines
  • Robotic movement fundamentals
Grade 7

App Design & Digital Interfaces

Discovering how everyday mobile software works by designing helpful touch apps, interfaces, and logic workflows.

  • User interface & interaction logic
  • Mobile variables & database storage
Grade 8

Python Logic & Automation

Transitioning into modern text-based syntax. Students learn structured programming, data loops, and scripted routines.

  • Variables, conditionals & loops
  • Automating everyday logical tasks
Grade 9

Connected Tech & Smart Systems

Understanding connected devices and modern automated systems using internet microcontrollers and smart sensors.

  • Connected environmental nodes
  • Smart automation architecture
